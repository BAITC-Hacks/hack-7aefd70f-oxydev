# Two-minute product demo

Open https://oxydev.govtech-kz.com before the call.

## 0:00–0:15

> Qadam AI is a multilingual assistant for the inVision U admissions team. It
> helps reveal an applicant's actions and potential behind the format of the
> response, while leaving the decision to a person.

Show EN / KK / RU and text, voice and video.

## 0:15–0:45

> An applicant responds in the most comfortable format. Instead of a long
> polished essay, Qadam asks for one concrete story and one short team scenario
> to reveal the reasoning process.

Choose **Write**, use the fictional example, select the first team option and
enter: `I would hear both people, agree on one owner for the next task, and
check progress today.` Confirm consent and open the reviewer workspace.

## 0:45–1:20

> The submission receives an anonymous reference and immediately enters the
> review queue. The reviewer sees the original evidence and follow-up questions.
> If evidence is missing, Qadam does not invent a confident score: a person
> selects the route and records a specific reason.

Show the `QDM-…` reference, original answer and decision panel.

## 1:20–1:45

> Text analysis follows ATOLA: Action, Thinking, Outcome, Learnings and
> Application. Every signal is linked to a verbatim quote. AI is not the judge;
> it structures evidence for a reviewer.

Show one ATOLA quote, then open **Methodology**.

## 1:45–2:00

> Qadam Evidence Framework adds nine versioned evidence blocks with consistent
> questions and observable anchors. Today the full journey works end to end:
> three languages, text, voice and video, review queue, explainable analysis and
> decision audit. The next step is expert calibration and a controlled pilot.
